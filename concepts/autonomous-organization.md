# Autonomous Organization

Per ADR-ES-021 section 1 + CR-ES-021 section 3.

## Canonical Definition

An Autonomous Organization is an Organization in which material organizational decisions, coordination, execution, or adaptation are capable of progressing independently within defined objectives, authority, policies, constraints, accountability, and governance boundaries without requiring human intervention for every organizational decision or action.

## Foundational Status

This concept is a specialization of the canonical Organization concept (per ADR-ES-022 + CR-ES-022).

## Canonical Relationships (per ADR-ES-021 section 9)

Autonomous Organization specializes Organization. Autonomous Organization operates-within Authority, governed-by Policy, constrained-by Constraint, pursues Organizational Objective, responds-to Organizational Context, produces Organizational Outcome, adapts-to Organizational Context, escalates-to Human or Authority.

Autonomous Organization uses Workflow, uses Agentic Workflow, uses Autonomous Operations, uses Agentic Operations, exercises Capability, contributes-to Value.

## Boundary Discipline (per ADR-ES-021 section 10)

Autonomous Organization is NOT Autonomous Enterprise, Autonomous Operations, Autonomous Service, Autonomous Product, Autonomous Offering, Autonomous Workflow, or Agentic Organization.

## Agentic/Autonomous Orthogonality (per ADR-ES-021 section 3 + section 8)

Four-state model:

- Conventional Organization (neither Agentic nor Autonomous)
- Agentic Organization (yes Agentic, no Autonomous)
- Autonomous Organization (no Agentic, yes Autonomous)
- Agentic + Autonomous Organization (both)

Neither dimension subsumes the other. An Organization may be both Agentic and Autonomous.
