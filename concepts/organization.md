# Organization

Per ADR-ES-022 section 1 + CR-ES-022 section 3.

## Canonical Definition

An Organization is a coordinated boundary of Actors (human and non-human), Roles, Responsibilities, Authority, Resources, and Activities that an Enterprise or organizational unit establishes to pursue Organizational Intent within defined Authority, Policy, Constraints, Governance, and Accountability, and that produces Organizational Outcomes.

## Foundational Status

This concept is foundational. It is the canonical grounding that ADR-ES-020 (Agentic Organization) specializes.

## Canonical Relationships (per ADR-ES-022 section 4)

Organization pursues Organizational Intent, coordinates Actors, allocates Roles + Responsibilities, delegates Authority, marshals Resources, undertakes Activities.

Organization operates-within Authority, governed-by Policy, constrained-by Constraint, operates-under Governance, accountable-under Accountability.

Organization produces Organizational Outcomes and escalates-through Escalation Boundary.

## Boundary Discipline (per ADR-ES-022 sections 5-9)

Organization is NOT Enterprise, Process, Activity, Capability, Value Stream, Operations, or Agent.

Enterprise, Process, Activity, Capability, Value Stream, Operations, and Agent are NOT Organization.

Team, Department, and Business Unit are sub-boundaries (deferred per ADR-ES-022 section 12).
