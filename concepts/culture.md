# Culture

Per ADR-ES-026 section 1 + CR-ES-026 section 2.

## Canonical Definition

Culture is the shared pattern of values, norms, beliefs, practices, expectations, and behavioral conventions through which an Organization shapes how its members and participants understand, coordinate, and conduct organizational activity.

## Foundational Status

Culture is a foundational concept within Enterprise-Semantics. It specializes Entity (not any existing concept).

## Canonical Relationships (per ADR-ES-026 section 5)

Culture specializes Entity. Culture supports Organization. Culture shapes Governance, Policy, Authority, Accountability, Capability, Process, Decision, Adaptation.

## Boundary Discipline (per ADR-ES-026 section 6 + Recon-ES-003)

Culture is NOT Organization, Governance, Policy, Process, Capability, Agentic Culture, Autonomous Culture, IT system.

## Properties (per ADR-ES-026 section 3 + CR-ES-026 section 3)

shared_values, shared_norms, shared_beliefs, shared_practices, shared_expectations, behavioral_conventions, interpretive_lens, coordination_of_meaning, organizational_scope, realization_mode.

## Architectural Significance

Per Recon-ES-003, Culture performs distinctive semantic work:
- Normative coherence
- Behavioral conventions
- Interpretive lens
- Coordination of meaning
- Values + beliefs + practices

These are NOT fully covered by Organization, Governance, Policy, Process, or Capability.
