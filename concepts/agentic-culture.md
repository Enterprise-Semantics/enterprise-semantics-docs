# Agentic Culture

Per ADR-ES-023 section 1 + CR-ES-023 section 3.

## Canonical Definition

A Culture in which organizational norms, values, practices, and expectations materially support the interpretation of delegated intent, distribution of decision authority, human-agent collaboration, adaptive action, and accountable coordination involving agentic behavior.

## Foundational Status

Agentic Culture is a specialization of Culture (canonical per ADR-ES-026 + CR-ES-026 Accepted).

## Canonical Relationships (per ADR-ES-023 section 7)

Agentic Culture specializes Culture. Agentic Culture supports Agentic Organization, Agentic Operations, Agentic Workflow. Agentic Culture engages-with Agent, responds-to Organizational Context, contributes-to Organizational Outcome. Agentic Culture shapes Delegation/Authority/Accountability/Collaboration/Decision Practice.

## Boundary Discipline (per ADR-ES-023 section 4 + section 8 + section 9 + section 10 + section 12)

Agentic Culture is NOT AI Culture, Automated Culture, Agentic Organization, Agentic Enterprise, Autonomous Culture, Human-free Culture, Agent-controlled Culture.

## Properties (per ADR-ES-023 section 4)

agentic_scope, cultural_context, delegation_norms, authority_norms, accountability_norms, collaboration_norms, decision_norms, adaptation_norms, trust_norms, intervention_norms, escalation_norms, learning_norms, governance_context, realization_mode.

## Orthogonality with Autonomous Culture (per ADR-ES-023 section 10 + ADR-ES-022 section 5)

Agentic Culture is ORTHOGONAL to Autonomous Culture. Neither subsumes the other.
