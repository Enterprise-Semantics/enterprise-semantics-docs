# Autonomous Culture

Per ADR-ES-024 section 1 + CR-ES-024 section 2.

## Canonical Definition

A Culture in which organizational norms, values, practices, and expectations materially support independent decision-making, delegated action, adaptive behavior, and reduced reliance on human intervention within defined objectives, authority, policies, constraints, accountability, and governance boundaries.

## Foundational Status

Autonomous Culture is a specialization of Culture (canonical per ADR-ES-026 + CR-ES-026 Accepted).

## Canonical Relationships (per ADR-ES-024 section 7)

Autonomous Culture specializes Culture. Autonomous Culture supports Autonomous/Agentic Organization, Autonomous/Agentic Operations. Autonomous Culture engages-with Agent, responds-to Organizational Context, contributes-to Organizational Outcome. Autonomous Culture shapes Delegation/Authority/Accountability/Intervention/Escalation/Decision/Adaptation Practice.

## Boundary Discipline (per ADR-ES-024 section 8)

Autonomous Culture is NOT Autonomous Organization, Autonomous Enterprise, Autonomous Operations, Autonomous Workflow, Autonomous Service, Autonomous Product, Autonomous Offering, Agentic Culture, AI Culture, Automated Culture.

## Properties (per CR-ES-024 section 3)

autonomy_scope, cultural_context, objective_norms, delegation_norms, authority_norms, decision_norms, action_norms, adaptation_norms, intervention_norms, escalation_norms, accountability_norms, trust_norms, governance_context, observation_norms, realization_mode.

## Orthogonality with Agentic Culture (per ADR-ES-024 section 5)

Autonomous Culture is ORTHOGONAL to Agentic Culture. Neither subsumes the other.
