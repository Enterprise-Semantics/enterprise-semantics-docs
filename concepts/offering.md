# Offering

Per ADR-ES-021 section 1 + CR-ES-021 section 3.

## Canonical Definition

An Offering is a coordinated bundle of Products, Services, and Capabilities that an enterprise or organizational unit commits to make available to a Customer (internal or external) in order to satisfy a defined Customer Need, within defined Authority, Policy, Constraints, and Governance, and that progresses through Offering Context Assessment, Configuration, Composition, Fulfillment, and Value Realization to produce Customer Outcome.

## Foundational Status

This concept is foundational. It is the canonical grounding that ADR-ES-018 (Agentic Offering) and ADR-ES-019 (Autonomous Offering) specialize.

## Canonical Relationships (per ADR-ES-021 section 4)

Offering addresses Customer Need.
Offering composes Product, Service, and exercises Capability.
Offering pursues Objective, responds-to Context, operates-within Authority, governed-by Policy, constrained-by Constraint.
Offering fulfilled-through Fulfillment Process, produces Customer Outcome, realizes Value, escalates-through Escalation Boundary.

## Boundary Discipline (per ADR-ES-021 sections 5-9)

Offering is NOT Product, Service, Capability, Value Stream, Enterprise, Customer Need, or Fulfillment Process.

Product, Service, Capability, Value Stream, Enterprise, Customer Need, and Fulfillment Process are NOT Offering.
