# Culture / Policy Boundary

Per ADR-ES-026 section 6 + CR-ES-026 section 7 + Recon-ES-003.

## Boundary Statement

Culture shapes Policy. Policy is a codified subset of cultural expectations. Culture is broader than Policy.

## Conformance

Reject:
- Culture = Policy
- Culture is-a Policy

unless separately authorized by a governed relationship.
