# Offering / Product Boundary

Per ADR-ES-021 section 5 + CR-ES-021 section 11.

## Boundary Statement

Offering composes Products. A Product is a constituent of an Offering, but an Offering is not a Product.

## Conformance

Reject:
- Offering is-a Product
- Product is-a Offering

unless separately authorized by a governed relationship.
