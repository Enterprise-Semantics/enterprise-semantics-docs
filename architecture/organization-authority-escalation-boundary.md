# Organization Authority Escalation Boundary

Per ADR-ES-020 section 13 and CR-ES-020 section 14 + section 16.

## Authority Boundaries

Per ADR-ES-020 section 13, an Agentic Organization operates within:
- Authority
- Policy
- Governance
- Constraints
- Accountability
- Escalation

## Escalation Chain

Per ADR-ES-020 section 13:

```
Intent
   |
   v
Delegation
   |
   v
Authority
   |
   v
Policy / Governance
   |
   v
Decision
   |
   v
Action
   |
   v
Outcome
   |
   v
Adaptation / Escalation
```

Agentic organizational behavior must remain bounded by organizational authority and governance.

## Intervention Models

Per ADR-ES-020 section 11 + CR-ES-020 section 16:

- human decision-makers
- human supervisors
- human escalation
- human-in-the-loop
- human-on-the-loop
- human-over-the-loop
- delegated agent participation
- mixed human/agent coordination

Agentic Organization does NOT require replacing human organizational actors.
