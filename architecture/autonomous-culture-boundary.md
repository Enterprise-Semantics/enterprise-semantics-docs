# Autonomous Culture Boundary

Per ADR-ES-024 + CR-ES-024 section 8.

## Boundary Statement

Autonomous Culture is a governed specialization of Culture that addresses cultural norms supporting bounded autonomous progression, separate from Autonomous Organization (operating model) and Autonomous Enterprise (cumulative realization).

## Boundary Identifiers

The Autonomous Culture boundary is identified by:
- objective norms
- delegation norms
- authority norms
- decision/action norms
- adaptation norms
- intervention/escalation norms
- accountability norms
- trust norms
- observation norms

## Boundary Constraints

The boundary must NOT be confused with:
- Autonomous Organization
- Autonomous Enterprise
- Autonomous Operations
- Autonomous Workflow
- Autonomous Service
- Autonomous Product
- Autonomous Offering
- Agentic Culture (orthogonal)
- AI Culture
- Automated Culture
