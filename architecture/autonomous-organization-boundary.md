# Autonomous Organization Boundary

Per ADR-ES-021 + CR-ES-021 section 12.

## Boundary Statement

The Autonomous Organization concept establishes a distinct semantic boundary at the level of organizational autonomy, distinct from the parent Organization concept.

## Boundary Identifiers

The Autonomous Organization boundary is identified by:
- the Organizational Objective it pursues
- the autonomy scope within defined governance
- the decision, action, coordination, and adaptation scopes
- the Authority, Policy, Constraints, Governance, and Accountability under which it operates
- the Organizational Outcomes it produces
- the escalation boundary to Human or Authority

## Boundary Constraints

The boundary must NOT be confused with:
- Autonomous Enterprise
- Autonomous Operations
- Autonomous Service
- Autonomous Product
- Autonomous Offering
- Autonomous Workflow
- Agentic Organization (orthogonal dimension)

Per ADR-ES-021 section 10.
