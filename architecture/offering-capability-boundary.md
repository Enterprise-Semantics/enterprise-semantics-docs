# Offering / Capability Boundary

Per ADR-ES-021 section 5 + CR-ES-021 section 11.

## Boundary Statement

Offering exercises Capability. A Capability is exercised by an Offering, but an Offering is not a Capability.

## Conformance

Reject:
- Offering is-a Capability
- Capability is-a Offering

unless separately authorized by a governed relationship.
