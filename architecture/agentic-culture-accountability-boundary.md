# Agentic Culture / Accountability Boundary

Per ADR-ES-023 section 11 + CR-ES-023 section 10.

## Boundary Statement

Agentic Culture supports accountability for agent-mediated decisions. Cultural norms require accountability for agent-mediated outcomes. Culture is NOT the accountability itself.

## Conformance

Reject:
- Agentic Culture removes accountability
- Accountability is-a Agentic Culture

unless separately authorized by a governed relationship.
