# Offering Autonomy Authority Boundary

Per ADR-ES-019 section 5 + section 7 and CR-ES-019 section 4 + section 7 + section 14.

## Authority Boundaries

Per ADR-ES-019 section 5, an Autonomous Offering operates within:
- Authority (decision authority)
- Policy (policy compliance)
- Constraints (constraint boundaries)
- Governance (governance boundaries)
- Escalation (escalation points)

## Escalation

Per ADR-ES-019 section 5 + CR-ES-019 section 4 + section 14:

Autonomous Offering MUST escalate when authority, policy, constraint, or governance boundaries are exceeded. Escalation is a first-class mechanism.

## Intervention Models

Per ADR-ES-019 section 7 + CR-ES-019 section 4 + section 14:

- approval gates
- human-in-the-loop
- human-on-the-loop
- human-over-the-loop
- exception intervention
- escalation
- supervisory governance

Autonomous does NOT mean human-free.
