# Offering / Autonomous Offering Realization Boundary

Per ADR-ES-019 section 1 + section 2 + section 3 and CR-ES-019 section 3 + section 13.

## Distinction

Offering represents what an organization makes available to a stakeholder or market as a coherent proposition. Autonomous Offering additionally describes how the offering is realized through autonomous material-realization within defined authority, policy, constraint, and governance boundaries.

The classification depends on whether the offering can progress independently through defined decisions, actions, coordination, and adaptation within specified offering objectives.

## Boundary Preservation

- Autonomous Offering retains the semantic identity of Offering (per ADR-ES-019 section 1 + AOFF-AUTO-CON-002)
- Autonomous Offering does NOT redefine Offering (per ADR-ES-019 section 1)
- Autonomous behavior is a contextual specialization, NOT a replacement

## Foundational Dependency

Per ADR-ES-019 section 2, this ADR does NOT establish the complete semantic definition of Offering. The parent Offering concept remains to be canonicalized via ADR-ES-019 (next-foundational tranche).

## Materiality

Per ADR-ES-019 section 6 + section 13 + CR-ES-019 section 8 + section 13:

- Insufficient: Offering + AI / Offering + automation / Offering + chatbot / Offering + Agent somewhere in implementation
- Potentially Autonomous: Offering + bounded authority + autonomous configuration/composition + autonomous coordination + autonomous adaptation + autonomous escalation
