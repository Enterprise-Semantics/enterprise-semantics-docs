# Offering Authority Escalation Boundary

Per ADR-ES-018 §5 + §9 and CR-ES-018 §4 + §7 + §14.

## Authority Boundaries

Per ADR-ES-018 §10, an Agentic Offering operates within:
- Authority (decision authority)
- Policy (policy compliance)
- Constraints (constraint boundaries)

## Escalation

Per ADR-ES-018 §5 + §11 + CR-ES-018 §4 + §14:

Agentic Offering MUST escalate when authority or governance boundaries are exceeded. Escalation is a first-class mechanism.

## Intervention Models

Per ADR-ES-018 §9 + CR-ES-018 §4 + §14:

- assisted interaction
- approval
- exception handling
- human escalation
- human oversight
- human-controlled boundaries

Agentic does NOT mean human-free.
