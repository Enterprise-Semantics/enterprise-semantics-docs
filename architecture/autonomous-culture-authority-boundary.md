# Autonomous Culture / Authority Boundary

Per ADR-ES-024 section 12 + CR-ES-024 section 8.

## Boundary Statement

Autonomous Culture is bounded by Authority + Policy + Constraint + Governance + Accountability + Escalation. Cultural acceptance of autonomy does NOT establish unrestricted authority.

## Conformance

Reject:
- Autonomous Culture has unlimited authority
- Autonomous Culture bypasses governance
- Autonomous Culture removes accountability

unless separately authorized by a governed relationship.
