# Organization Boundary

Per ADR-ES-022 + CR-ES-022 section 11.

## Boundary Statement

The Organization concept establishes a distinct semantic boundary at the level of a coordinated boundary of actors, roles, responsibilities, authority, resources, and activities that pursues Organizational Intent within defined governance boundaries.

## Boundary Identifiers

The Organization boundary is identified by:
- the Organizational Intent it pursues
- the Actors it coordinates (human + non-human)
- the Roles + Responsibilities it allocates
- the Authority it delegates
- the Resources it marshals
- the Activities it undertakes
- the Policy, Constraints, Governance, and Accountability under which it operates
- the Organizational Outcomes it produces

## Boundary Constraints

The boundary must NOT be confused with:
- an Enterprise
- a Process
- an Activity
- a Capability
- a Value Stream
- Operations
- an Agent
- a Team (sub-boundary)
- a Department (sub-boundary)
- a Business Unit (sub-boundary)

Per ADR-ES-022 section 11 (Rejected Interpretations).
