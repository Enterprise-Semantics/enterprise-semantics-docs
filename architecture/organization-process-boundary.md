# Organization / Process Boundary

Per ADR-ES-022 section 7 + CR-ES-022 section 11.

## Boundary Statement

Organization coordinates Processes. An Organization undertakes Processes, but an Organization is not a Process.

## Conformance

Reject:
- Organization is-a Process
- Process is-a Organization

unless separately authorized by a governed relationship.
