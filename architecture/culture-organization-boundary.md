# Culture / Organization Boundary

Per ADR-ES-026 section 6 + CR-ES-026 section 7 + Recon-ES-003.

## Boundary Statement

Culture supports Organization. Culture shapes how Organization is interpreted, coordinated, and conducted. Culture is NOT Organization.

## Conformance

Reject:
- Culture is-a Organization
- Organization is-a Culture

unless separately authorized by a governed relationship.
