# Organization / Capability Boundary

Per ADR-ES-022 section 9 + CR-ES-022 section 11.

## Boundary Statement

Organization exercises Capability. A Capability is exercised by an Organization, but an Organization is not a Capability.

## Conformance

Reject:
- Organization is-a Capability
- Capability is-a Organization

unless separately authorized by a governed relationship.
