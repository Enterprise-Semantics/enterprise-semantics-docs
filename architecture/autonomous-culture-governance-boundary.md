# Autonomous Culture / Governance Boundary

Per ADR-ES-024 section 12 + CR-ES-024 section 8.

## Boundary Statement

Autonomous Culture retains Governance. Cultural autonomy is bounded by organizational Governance ; autonomy does NOT imply governance-free operation.

## Conformance

Reject:
- Autonomous Culture has no governance
- Governance is-a Autonomous Culture

unless separately authorized by a governed relationship.
