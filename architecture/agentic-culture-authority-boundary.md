# Agentic Culture / Authority Boundary

Per ADR-ES-023 section 13 + CR-ES-023 section 10.

## Boundary Statement

Agentic Culture is bounded by Authority + Policy + Constraint + Governance + Accountability + Escalation. Cultural acceptance of delegation does NOT establish unrestricted authority.

## Conformance

Reject:
- Agentic Culture has unlimited authority
- Agentic Culture bypasses governance
- Agentic Culture removes accountability

unless separately authorized by a governed relationship.
