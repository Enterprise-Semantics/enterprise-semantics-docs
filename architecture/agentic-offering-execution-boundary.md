# Agentic Offering Execution Boundary

Per ADR-ES-018 §11 and CR-ES-018 §3 + §7.

## Pattern

```
Stakeholder Intent
   |
   v
Agentic Offering
   |
   v
Interpret Context
   |
   v
Configure / Compose
   |
   v
Select / Coordinate
   |
   v
Fulfill
   |
   v
Observe Outcome
   |
   v
Adapt / Escalate
   <loop>
```

## Mixed Realization

Per ADR-ES-018 §9, the entire offering does NOT need to operate agentically. An Agentic Offering may employ conventional, automated, human-mediated, and agentic realization mechanisms simultaneously.
