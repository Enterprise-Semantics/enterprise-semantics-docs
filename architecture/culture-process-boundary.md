# Culture / Process Boundary

Per ADR-ES-026 section 6 + CR-ES-026 section 7 + Recon-ES-003.

## Boundary Statement

Culture shapes Process. Culture is how Process is interpreted and conducted. Culture is NOT Process.

## Conformance

Reject:
- Culture is-a Process
- Process is-a Culture

unless separately authorized by a governed relationship.
