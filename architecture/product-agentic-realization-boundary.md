# Product / Agentic Product Realization Boundary

Per ADR-ES-016 §1 + §2 + §3 and CR-ES-016 §3 + §5.

## Distinction

Product describes something intentionally offered to a stakeholder or consumer for the realization of intended value. Agentic Product additionally describes how that product is realized through material agentic behavior within defined authority, policy, and contextual boundaries.

The classification depends on where the material agentic behavior resides.

## Boundary Preservation

- Agentic Product retains the semantic identity of Product (per ADR-ES-016 §4 + APROD-CON-002)
- Agentic Product does NOT redefine Product (per ADR-ES-016 §1)
- Agentic behavior is a contextual specialization, NOT a replacement

## Product Universal Semantics

Per ADR-ES-002 + ADR-ES-016 §3:

- Product purpose
- Product intent
- Product value orientation
- Product behavior

Agentic Product preserves all Product universal semantics.

## Materiality

Per ADR-ES-016 §6 + CR-ES-016 §27:

- Insufficient: Product + AI component / Product + automated workflow / Product + Agent invocation
- Potentially Agentic: Product + delegated intent + contextual interpretation + bounded authority + dynamic action selection + outcome-oriented adaptation
