# Culture / Capability Boundary

Per ADR-ES-026 section 6 + CR-ES-026 section 7 + Recon-ES-003.

## Boundary Statement

Culture shapes Capability. Culture is how Capability is exercised. Culture is NOT Capability.

## Conformance

Reject:
- Culture is-a Capability
- Capability is-a Culture

unless separately authorized by a governed relationship.
