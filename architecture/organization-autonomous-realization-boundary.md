# Organization / Autonomous Organization Realization Boundary

Per ADR-ES-021 section 1 + section 6 + CR-ES-021 section 12.

## Boundary Statement

Autonomous Organization is the realization of the parent Organization concept under material organizational autonomy. The autonomy must apply to organizational decision, coordination, execution, adaptation, or progression, not merely to constituent components.

## Autonomy Materiality

Material organizational autonomy may include:

1. independent organizational decision execution
2. independent action execution
3. autonomous coordination
4. contextual organizational response
5. autonomous exception handling
6. autonomous adaptation
7. autonomous progression toward organizational objectives
8. bounded escalation when authority is exceeded

The following alone SHALL NOT establish autonomy: AI, automation, autonomous software, autonomous service, autonomous product, autonomous offering, autonomous operations, Agent, Agentic Workflow.

Per ADR-ES-021 section 5 + section 11.
