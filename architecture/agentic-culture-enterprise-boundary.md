# Agentic Culture / Agentic Enterprise Boundary

Per ADR-ES-023 section 9 + CR-ES-023 section 10.

## Boundary Statement

Agentic Culture is NOT Agentic Enterprise. Enterprise concerns cumulative priority realization ; Culture concerns shared meaning + norms.

## Conformance

Reject:
- Agentic Culture is-a Agentic Enterprise
- Agentic Enterprise is-a Agentic Culture

unless separately authorized by a governed relationship.
