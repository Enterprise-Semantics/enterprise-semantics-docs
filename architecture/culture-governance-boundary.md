# Culture / Governance Boundary

Per ADR-ES-026 section 6 + CR-ES-026 section 7 + Recon-ES-003.

## Boundary Statement

Culture shapes Governance. Culture is the shared meaning that legitimizes and modifies how Governance is exercised. Culture is NOT Governance.

## Conformance

Reject:
- Culture is-a Governance
- Governance is-a Culture

unless separately authorized by a governed relationship.
