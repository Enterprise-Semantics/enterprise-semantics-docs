# Autonomous Culture / Intervention Boundary

Per ADR-ES-024 section 6 + CR-ES-024 section 8.

## Boundary Statement

Autonomous Culture retains Intervention. Cultural autonomy allows exception-based human intervention ; autonomy does NOT imply human-free operation.

## Conformance

Reject:
- Autonomous Culture prohibits human intervention
- Autonomous Culture has no escalation

unless separately authorized by a governed relationship.
