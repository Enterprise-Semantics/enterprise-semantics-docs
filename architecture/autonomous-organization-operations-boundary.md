# Autonomous Organization / Autonomous Operations Boundary

Per ADR-ES-021 section 10 + CR-ES-021 section 12.

## Boundary Statement

Autonomous Organization uses Autonomous Operations as a constituent. An autonomous operations environment may support an Autonomous Organization, but the Organization is not Autonomous Operations.

## Conformance

Reject:
- Autonomous Organization is-a Autonomous Operations
- Autonomous Operations is-a Autonomous Organization

unless separately authorized by a governed relationship.
