# Culture Boundary

Per ADR-ES-026 + CR-ES-026 section 7.

## Boundary Statement

Culture is a foundational concept with a distinct semantic boundary at the level of organizational shared meaning, separate from Organization, Governance, Policy, Process, and Capability.

## Boundary Identifiers

The Culture boundary is identified by:
- shared values + norms + beliefs
- shared practices + expectations
- behavioral conventions
- interpretive lens
- coordination of meaning

## Boundary Constraints (per ADR-ES-026 section 6)

The boundary must NOT be confused with:
- Organization (structural)
- Governance (formal framework)
- Policy (codified subset)
- Process (activity sequence)
- Capability (functional ability)
- Agentic Culture (specialization)
- Autonomous Culture (specialization)
