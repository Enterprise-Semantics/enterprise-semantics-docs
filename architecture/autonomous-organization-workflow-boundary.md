# Autonomous Organization / Workflow Boundary

Per ADR-ES-021 section 10 + CR-ES-021 section 12.

## Boundary Statement

Autonomous Organization uses Workflow and Agentic Workflow as constituents. An autonomous organizational behavior may employ workflows, but the Organization is not a workflow.

## Conformance

Reject:
- Autonomous Organization is-a Workflow
- Autonomous Organization is-a Agentic Workflow

unless separately authorized by a governed relationship.
