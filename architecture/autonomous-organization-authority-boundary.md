# Autonomous Organization / Authority Boundary

Per ADR-ES-021 section 12 + CR-ES-021 section 12.

## Boundary Statement

Autonomous Organization operates within defined Authority. Organizational autonomy is bounded by Authority; autonomy does not imply unrestricted discretion.

## Bounded Authority

Autonomous Organization SHALL retain:

- Defined Objectives
- Defined Authority
- Policy
- Constraints
- Accountability
- Governance
- Escalation
- Observation

Autonomy SHALL NOT imply:

- Unlimited Authority
- No Governance
- No Accountability
- No Human Oversight
- Unrestricted Decision Rights
