# Agentic Culture Boundary

Per ADR-ES-023 + CR-ES-023 section 10.

## Boundary Statement

Agentic Culture is a governed specialization of Culture that addresses cultural norms supporting agentic behavior, separate from Agentic Organization (operating model) and Agentic Enterprise (cumulative realization).

## Boundary Identifiers

The Agentic Culture boundary is identified by:
- delegation norms
- authority norms
- accountability norms
- collaboration norms
- decision norms
- adaptation norms
- trust norms
- intervention norms
- escalation norms
- learning norms

## Boundary Constraints

The boundary must NOT be confused with:
- AI Culture
- Automated Culture
- Agentic Organization
- Agentic Enterprise
- Agentic Operations
- Agentic Workflow
- Autonomous Culture (orthogonal)
- Human-free Culture
- Agent-controlled Culture
