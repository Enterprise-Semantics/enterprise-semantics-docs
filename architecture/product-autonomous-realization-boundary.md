# Product / Autonomous Product Realization Boundary

Per ADR-ES-017 §1 + §2 + §3 and CR-ES-017 §2 + §5.

## Distinction

Product describes something intentionally offered to a stakeholder or consumer for the realization of intended value. Autonomous Product additionally describes how that product is realized with bounded independent progression within product objectives, authority, policies, constraints, and governance boundaries.

The classification depends on where the material autonomous progression resides.

## Boundary Preservation

- Autonomous Product retains the semantic identity of Product (per ADR-ES-017 §1 + APROD-AUTO-CON-002)
- Autonomous Product does NOT redefine Product (per ADR-ES-017 §1)
- Autonomous progression is a contextual specialization, NOT a replacement

## Product Universal Semantics

Per ADR-ES-002 + ADR-ES-017 §3:

- Product purpose
- Product intent
- Product value orientation
- Product behavior

Autonomous Product preserves all Product universal semantics.

## Materiality

Per ADR-ES-017 §5 + CR-ES-017 §8:

- Insufficient: Product + software / Product + APIs / Product + automation / Product + AI / Product + Agent / Product + machine learning / Product + event-driven execution
- Potentially Autonomous: Product + defined product objective + bounded decision/action scope + authority/policy/constraints/governance fences + adaptation + escalation + outcome observation
