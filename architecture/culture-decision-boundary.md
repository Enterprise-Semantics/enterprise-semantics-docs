# Culture / Decision Boundary

Per ADR-ES-026 section 5 + CR-ES-026 section 7.

## Boundary Statement

Culture shapes Decision. Culture is the shared meaning through which Decisions are interpreted and made. Culture is NOT Decision itself.

## Conformance

Reject:
- Culture is-a Decision
- Decision is-a Culture

unless separately authorized by a governed relationship.
