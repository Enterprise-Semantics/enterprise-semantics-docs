# Organization / Operations Boundary

Per CR-ES-022 section 11.

## Boundary Statement

Organization operates within Operations boundary. An Organization may operate through Operations, but an Organization is not Operations.

## Conformance

Reject:
- Organization is-a Operations
- Operations is-a Organization

unless separately authorized by a governed relationship.
