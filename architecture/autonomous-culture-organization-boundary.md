# Autonomous Culture / Autonomous Organization Boundary

Per ADR-ES-024 section 8 + CR-ES-024 section 8.

## Boundary Statement

Autonomous Culture supports Autonomous Organization. Culture is the shared meaning supporting bounded autonomous progression ; Organization is the entity that operates autonomously. Culture is NOT Organization.

## Conformance

Reject:
- Autonomous Culture is-a Autonomous Organization
- Autonomous Organization is-a Autonomous Culture

unless separately authorized by a governed relationship.
