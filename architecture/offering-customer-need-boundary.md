# Offering / Customer Need Boundary

Per ADR-ES-021 section 8 + CR-ES-021 section 11.

## Boundary Statement

Offering addresses Customer Need. The Customer Need is what the Offering addresses, but an Offering is not a Customer Need.

## Conformance

Reject:
- Offering is-a Customer Need
- Customer Need is-a Offering

unless separately authorized by a governed relationship.
