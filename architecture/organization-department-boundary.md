# Organization / Department Boundary

Per ADR-ES-022 section 11 + section 12.

## Boundary Statement

A Department is a sub-boundary within Organization. Department is not equivalent to Organization; Department is deferred per ADR-ES-022 section 12.

## Conformance

This CR does NOT establish Department as a foundational concept. The boundary documentation establishes that Department is a sub-boundary concept that requires independent semantic governance.
