# Offering / Service Boundary

Per ADR-ES-021 section 5 + CR-ES-021 section 11.

## Boundary Statement

Offering composes Services. A Service is a constituent of an Offering, but an Offering is not a Service.

## Conformance

Reject:
- Offering is-a Service
- Service is-a Offering

unless separately authorized by a governed relationship.
