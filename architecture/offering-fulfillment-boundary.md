# Offering / Fulfillment Process Boundary

Per ADR-ES-021 section 9 + CR-ES-021 section 11.

## Boundary Statement

Offering fulfilled-through Fulfillment Process. The Fulfillment Process is what fulfills the Offering, but an Offering is not a Fulfillment Process.

## Conformance

Reject:
- Offering is-a Fulfillment Process
- Fulfillment Process is-a Offering

unless separately authorized by a governed relationship.
