# Organization / Team Boundary

Per ADR-ES-022 section 11 + section 12.

## Boundary Statement

A Team is a sub-boundary within Organization. Team is not equivalent to Organization; Team is deferred per ADR-ES-022 section 12.

## Conformance

This CR does NOT establish Team as a foundational concept. The boundary documentation establishes that Team is a sub-boundary concept that requires independent semantic governance.
