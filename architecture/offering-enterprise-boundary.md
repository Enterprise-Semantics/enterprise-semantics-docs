# Offering / Enterprise Boundary

Per ADR-ES-021 section 7 + CR-ES-021 section 11.

## Boundary Statement

Enterprise offers Offerings. An Offering is offered by an Enterprise, but an Offering is not an Enterprise.

## Conformance

Reject:
- Offering is-a Enterprise
- Enterprise is-a Offering

unless separately authorized by a governed relationship.
