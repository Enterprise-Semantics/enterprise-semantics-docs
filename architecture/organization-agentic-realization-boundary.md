# Organization / Agentic Organization Realization Boundary

Per ADR-ES-020 section 1 + section 2 + section 3 and CR-ES-020 section 3 + section 8.

## Distinction

Organization represents the boundary for coordinated actors, roles, responsibilities, authority, resources, and activities. Agentic Organization additionally describes how material organizational activities incorporate agentic behavior to interpret delegated intent, select or coordinate actions, adapt to context, or pursue organizational outcomes.

The classification depends on whether material agentic behavior affects organizational decision-making, coordination, execution, adaptation, delegation, exception management, or organizational response.

## Boundary Preservation

- Agentic Organization retains the semantic identity of Organization (per ADR-ES-020 section 1 + AORG-CON-002)
- Agentic Organization does NOT redefine Organization (per ADR-ES-020 section 1)
- Agentic behavior is a contextual specialization, NOT a replacement

## Foundational Dependency

Per ADR-ES-020 section 2, this ADR does NOT establish the complete semantic definition of Organization. The parent Organization concept remains to be canonicalized via ADR-ES-021 (next-foundational tranche).

## Materiality

Per ADR-ES-020 section 3 + CR-ES-020 section 8:

- Insufficient: Organization + Agent / Organization + AI / Organization + automation
- Potentially Agentic: Organization + delegated organizational intent + contextual interpretation + dynamic organizational decisions + action selection + coordination + adaptation + exception interpretation + organizational escalation
