# Product Authority Escalation Boundary

Per ADR-ES-016 §6 + §8 and CR-ES-016 §4 + §5 + §12.

## Authority Boundaries

Per ADR-ES-016 §6, an Agentic Product operates within:

- Authority (decision authority)
- Policy (policy compliance)
- Constraints (constraint boundaries)
- Governance (governance framework)

## Escalation

Per ADR-ES-016 §6 + §8 + §15 + CR-ES-016 §4 + §12:

Agentic Product MUST escalate when authority or governance boundaries are exceeded. Escalation is a first-class mechanism.

## Intervention Models

Per ADR-ES-016 §15 + CR-ES-016 §4 + §12:

- human-in-the-loop
- human-on-the-loop
- human-over-the-loop
- approval gates
- exception-based intervention
- escalation
- human-controlled boundaries
