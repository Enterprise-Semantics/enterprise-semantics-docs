# Product Autonomy Authority Boundary

Per ADR-ES-017 §4 + §6 and CR-ES-017 §3 + §6 + §10.

## Authority Boundaries

Per ADR-ES-017 §4, an Autonomous Product operates within:

1. Product Objective (product purpose)
2. Authority (decision authority)
3. Policy (policy compliance)
4. Constraints (constraint boundaries)
5. Governance (governance framework)
6. Escalation (escalation conditions)

## Escalation

Per ADR-ES-017 §4 + §6 + CR-ES-017 §3 + §10:

Autonomous Product MUST escalate when authority or governance boundaries are exceeded. Escalation is a first-class mechanism.

## Intervention Models

Per ADR-ES-017 §6 + CR-ES-017 §3 + §10 + APROD-AUTO-CON-014:

- human-in-the-loop
- human-on-the-loop
- human-over-the-loop
- approval gates
- exception-based intervention
- escalation
- supervisory governance

Autonomous Product does NOT mean human-free, unattended, uncontrolled, governance-free, unlimited, irreversible.
