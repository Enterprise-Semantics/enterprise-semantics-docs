# Offering Boundary

Per ADR-ES-021 + CR-ES-021 section 11.

## Boundary Statement

The Offering concept establishes a distinct semantic boundary at the level of a coordinated bundle that addresses Customer Need through configuration, composition, fulfillment, and value realization.

## Boundary Identifiers

The Offering boundary is identified by:
- the Customer Need it addresses
- the coordinated bundle of Products + Services + Capabilities it composes
- the Customer Outcome it produces
- the Value it realizes
- the Authority, Policy, Constraints, and Governance under which it operates

## Boundary Constraints

The boundary must NOT be confused with:
- a single Product
- a single Service
- a single Capability
- the Value Stream that may involve the Offering
- the Enterprise that offers the Offering
- the Customer Need that the Offering addresses
