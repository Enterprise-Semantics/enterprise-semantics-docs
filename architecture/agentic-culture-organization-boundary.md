# Agentic Culture / Agentic Organization Boundary

Per ADR-ES-023 section 8 + CR-ES-023 section 10.

## Boundary Statement

Agentic Culture supports Agentic Organization. Culture is the shared meaning through which organizational behavior operates. Organization is the entity whose behavior operates. Culture is NOT Organization.

## Conformance

Reject:
- Agentic Culture is-a Agentic Organization
- Agentic Organization is-a Agentic Culture

unless separately authorized by a governed relationship.
