# Autonomous Culture / Autonomous Enterprise Boundary

Per ADR-ES-024 section 8 + CR-ES-024 section 8.

## Boundary Statement

Autonomous Culture is NOT Autonomous Enterprise. Enterprise concerns cumulative priority realization ; Culture concerns shared meaning + norms.

## Conformance

Reject:
- Autonomous Culture is-a Autonomous Enterprise
- Autonomous Enterprise is-a Autonomous Culture

unless separately authorized by a governed relationship.
