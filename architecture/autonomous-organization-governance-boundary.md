# Autonomous Organization / Governance Boundary

Per ADR-ES-021 section 12 + CR-ES-021 section 12.

## Boundary Statement

Autonomous Organization retains Governance. Organizational autonomy is bounded by organizational Governance; autonomy does not imply governance-free operation.

## Governance Retention

Autonomy is bounded by:

- Organizational Objectives
- Authority
- Policies
- Constraints
- Accountability
- Governance
- Escalation

The autonomous progression operates within these boundaries and escalates when authority is exceeded.
